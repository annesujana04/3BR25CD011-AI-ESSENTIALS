# Repository Overview

This repository contains materials related to coding assignments and resumes.

## Contents

*   **`day2-coding-assignment.md`**: Contains a link to [Rocket Run](https://rocket-run-eight.vercel.app), a web-based Deep Space Survival game.
    *   **About the Game**: "Thread the asteroid belt, harvest energy before your reactor runs dry, and push your velocity as far as you dare."
    *   **Controls**:
        *   **Steer**: `←`/`→` arrows or `A`/`D` keys.
        *   **Boost**: `Space` or `Shift`.
        *   **Touch Controls**: Drag to steer, hold BOOST to accelerate.
    *   **Gameplay Mechanics**:
        *   Collect **Energy cells** to refuel (+20) and increase your score (+50).
        *   Avoid **Asteroids** as impacts cost one life.
*   **`resume.pdf`**: A resume document.
*   **`sujana.pdf`**: Another resume document (Sujana).
